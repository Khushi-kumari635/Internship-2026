About AlumniConnect

Bridging the gap between students and alumni, one connection at a time.

Our Mission

AlumniConnect was built to solve a simple but important problem — after graduation, most alumni lose touch with their college, and current students lose access to the valuable guidance, mentorship, and opportunities that alumni could offer. Our platform creates a single, easy-to-use space where students and alumni can find each other, connect, and grow together.

What We Offer

From a searchable alumni directory and a dedicated job board to event management with RSVP tracking and direct messaging, AlumniConnect brings together everything a college community needs to stay engaged — long after graduation day.

Why We Built This

This project was developed as a B.Tech Computer Science college project, demonstrating a complete full-stack web application using HTML, CSS, JavaScript on the frontend and Node.js, Express.js, and MongoDB on the backend. It showcases real-world features like authentication, RESTful APIs, dynamic filtering, and responsive design — all built with clean, well-commented, beginner-friendly code.
